Projects

Each card says honestly where the project stands.

  • Hydroboro

    Oct 2025 In progress

    A research and product venture for measuring how systems behave over time. It looks at a stream of data and asks whether its behavior looks like noise, a persistent pattern, or a change in operating conditions (a “regime change”). It spans software observability tools, an interface, offline demonstrations and proposed physical instruments, with reproducible evidence and traceable records at its center. In everyday terms: a dashboard that helps you understand what a system is doing, and what evidence supports that view.
